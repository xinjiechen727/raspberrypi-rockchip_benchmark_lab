What the numbers do (and don't) prove
  • Throughput: median of three previously reported trials, not raw samples in the two latest attached logs.
  • 2×4K60: two separate 60-second runs per device, each with 7,200 total delivered frames.
  • 4×4K60: RK ran for 60 seconds; Pi 5 was intentionally stopped after a 10-second overload challenge.
  • All throughput and parallel data are decoded-frame delivery measurements; no HDMI presentation or display-refresh metrics.
  • 7.59 and 2.75 are fps/60 equivalents only; they are not validated multi-stream concurrency limits.
  • Not a 30-minute stability test. No pixel-level correctness, system power, temperatures or cost normalization reported.
  • RK MPP emitted recurring HEVC SEI parse warnings. The latest tested streams still delivered every expected frame.
